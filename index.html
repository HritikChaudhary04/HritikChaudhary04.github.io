Hritik Chaudhary

BSc Computational Mathematics, Kathmandu University · Kathmandu, Nepal

I work on queueing theory and applied probability, mainly matrix-analytic methods: quasi-birth-and-death (QBD) processes, Markovian arrival processes (MAPs) and phase-type distributions.

My undergraduate thesis modeled transaction confirmation time in a permissioned central bank digital currency (CBDC) system. I checked the model against a SimPy simulation and a Hyperledger Fabric prototype. I have since rebuilt the model stage by stage, added matrix-analytic methods for bursty traffic, and checked it on a five-institution Fabric testbed. I am also interested in game theory, in particular bargaining problems where the arrivals follow a MAP.

I graduated in July 2026 with a CGPA of 3.90/4.00. Since 2022 I have also taught A-Level mathematics, both at home and online.

I am looking for a funded PhD position starting in 2027. If my work fits your group, please email me.
